I'm a Senior Software Engineer focused on building scalable systems and solving complex engineering problems.

I've spent my career working with startups, giving me the opportunity to work across the entire stack, from architecture and data modeling to APIs, frontend applications, third-party integrations, and production deployments.

MongoDB has become a major part of my professional journey. I'm a MongoDB Champion and MongoDB Subject Matter Expert, and I enjoy working on problems around data modeling, performance, search, vector search, and building production systems around real-world data.

I'm also heavily involved in the developer community. I'm ranked among the top 0.03% of Stack Overflow users, lead the MongoDB User Group Belgrade, organize workshops and events, speak at conferences and podcasts, and share practical engineering knowledge through my blog, [Code Beyond Limits](https://codebeyondlimits.com/).
